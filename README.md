# USBStat

A lightweight macOS **menu bar** utility that gives you an instant overview of every USB device connected to your Mac — and lets you eject them safely in one click.

![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-macOS%2013%2B-blue)
![Made with](https://img.shields.io/badge/made%20with-SwiftUI-orange)

## Features

- 🔌 **Device overview** — see all connected USB devices with name, vendor and serial number
- ⚡ **Technical details** — USB version, transfer speed and power draw at a glance
- 💾 **Live disk usage** — used, free and percentage for every mounted volume, updated in real time
- ⏏️ **Safe eject** — unmount and eject any volume with one click, with a confirmation notification
- 📂 **Reveal in Finder** — jump straight to a mounted volume
- 🔔 **Connect / disconnect notifications** — always know what just got plugged in or removed
- 🚀 **Launch at Login** — optional, controlled by a single toggle

## Privacy

USBStat runs **entirely on your Mac**. It does **not** collect, transmit or store any of your data, makes **no network connections**, and requires **no account**. The app is fully sandboxed.

## Requirements

- macOS 13.0 (Ventura) or later

## Usage

1. Click the USBStat icon in the menu bar to open the panel.
2. Connect a USB device — it appears in the list with its details.
3. For mounted volumes you'll see live disk usage, an **Eject** button and a **Reveal in Finder** button.
4. Toggle **Launch at Login** at the bottom of the panel to start USBStat automatically.
5. Right-click the menu bar icon to **Quit**.
