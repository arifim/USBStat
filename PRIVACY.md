# Privacy Policy

**USBStat**

_Last updated: June 2026_

## Overview

USBStat is a macOS menu bar utility that displays information about USB devices connected to your Mac. Your privacy is important, and this policy explains how the app handles data.

## Data Collection

**USBStat does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

- The app runs entirely on your Mac.
- It makes **no network connections** of any kind.
- It does **not** use analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party services.
- It does **not** require an account or any sign-in.

## Device Information

USBStat reads metadata about connected USB devices (such as device name, vendor, serial number, transfer speed, and disk usage) using Apple's public IOKit and DiskArbitration frameworks. This information is displayed only on your own Mac, in the app's interface, and is **never sent anywhere**.

## Notifications

If you grant notification permission, USBStat shows local notifications when a device is connected, disconnected, or ejected. These notifications are generated entirely on your device.
